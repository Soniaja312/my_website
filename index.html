Sonia, to those
who know me.

Welcome to my corner of the internet—a digital archive of projects and side quests that I'm constantly learning from. At Columbia University, I'm studying Mechanical Engineering and Entrepreneurship while conducting fusion research and competing on the Columbia Fencing team. I'm fascinated by the intersection of climate and deep tech, especially where AI, data centers, and carbon technologies collide. I don't think climate investing and cutting-edge innovation should be mutually exclusive. Along the way, I love connecting with people through photography, cooking, building websites and apps, writing on Substack, and collecting stories from the people and places I encounter.
